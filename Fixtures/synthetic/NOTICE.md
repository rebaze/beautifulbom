# Synthetic SBOMs

Written by hand for the tests: CycloneDX 1.6 documents with nested components and the edge cases
of the TR-03183-2 rules, and SPDX 2.3 and 3.0 documents (`shop-2.3.spdx.json`, `shop-3.0.spdx.json`) with every
relationship the SPDX reading handles. "Acme Devices" and its components are invented; real package names
(openssl, curl, zlib) appear only as names, with no data from any product.
