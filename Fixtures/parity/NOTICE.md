# rio's results

The output of rio (https://github.com/rebaze/rio, Apache License 2.0) for the fixtures in
`../public/`, `../rio/`, `../synthetic/` and `../tools/` (CycloneDX only), one `<fixture>.rio.json` each. The parity tests
compare the app's TR-03183-2 checks with them; `make parity-refresh` regenerates them with the
`rio` on your PATH.
